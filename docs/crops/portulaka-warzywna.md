# Portulaka warzywna

## Role in the shallow-planter plan

Portulaka is the principal summer crop in the suitable shallow-planter modules. The plan selected it for its fit with heat, shallow substrate and repeated harvesting.

## Sowing pattern

**3×3 = 9 clumps, 2–4 seeds/point, 2–4 mm deep → keep 1–2 strongest**

## Harvest

- Regularly shorten young shoot tips, similarly to basil.
- Normally harvest the terminal **4–7 cm of shoot**.
- Leave several leaves/nodes and some side branching below the cut.
- Do not remove the entire green mass at once.
- At the end of the season, take a full harvest and clear the module for the winter crop.
