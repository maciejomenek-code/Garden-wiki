# Klajtonia

## Role in the shallow-planter plan

- Main autumn–winter–spring crop.
- Standard autumn start: approximately **ww38**.
- Preferred where strong regrowth in late winter and spring is important.

## Sowing pattern

**3×3 = 9 clumps, 2–3 seeds/point, 5–6 mm deep → keep 1–2 strongest**

## Harvest

- Harvest more assertively in autumn and spring.
- In winter, prefer individual large leaves.
- When cutting a whole clump, leave approximately **2–3 cm above the soil** and avoid damaging the growth centre.
- At the end of the phase, a full harvest can be taken and roots removed.

See the [6 cm shallow-planter rotation](../planning/shallow-planters-6cm.md).
