# Roszponka

## Role in the shallow-planter plan

- Main autumn–winter–spring crop.
- Standard autumn start: approximately **ww36**.
- Preferred where a classic winter rosette and strong cold tolerance are priorities.
- The plan explicitly allows a larger number of winter roszponka modules.

## Sowing pattern

**3×3 = 9 plants, 2–3 seeds/point, 5–6 mm deep → keep 1 strongest**

## Harvest

- Primarily take the largest **outer leaves**, leaving the centre of the rosette.
- Harvest more conservatively in winter.
- Cut a whole rosette mainly for the final harvest or when a larger one-off harvest is needed.
